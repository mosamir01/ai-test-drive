AI Test Drive

New AI tools, tested by an AI agent.

AI Test Drive is a YouTube channel about brand-new AI tools. For each video, an AI agent uses the tool in a real web browser while the screen is recorded, and an AI voice explains what happens. Every video says so in its description.

The videos are published by shortsfactory, the channel's own publishing tool. It uses the YouTube API only to upload and schedule the channel's videos and to read their statistics.
